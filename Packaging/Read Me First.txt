MICFLOW

Start here:  double-click "Start Here - Install Guide" to open the step-by-step guide
(https://micflow.abdullahel.com/guide.html).

Quick version
  1. Drag MicFlow onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open MicFlow. Your Mac blocks apps it doesn't know the first time: click Done, then
     open System Settings > Privacy & Security, find "MicFlow was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
     You only do this once.
  3. Allow Microphone, Accessibility and Screen Recording when asked.
  4. Voice typing: follow the three steps on the first page to add your free API key.

The microphone and speaker boost need two small sound helpers. MicFlow downloads them for you
when you turn a boost on (Sound page); you restart your Mac once.

MicFlow is free software, available only from https://micflow.abdullahel.com.
